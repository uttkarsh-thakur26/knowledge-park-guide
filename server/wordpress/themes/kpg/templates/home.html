All guides

Every guide on Knowledge Park Guide, newest first. Each one answers a question students ask when they first arrive in Knowledge Park, Greater Noida: which metro station to use, how far it is to campus, what an auto should cost, and who to contact at each college. Fares, distances and timings come from official sources such as NMRC or from my own trips, and every fare table says when it was last checked.

The guides are grouped by topic. Getting here covers the metro, autos, e-rickshaws and buses, and Living covers college contacts and day-to-day life. Guides on places to stay, food and things to do are next. If something here is out of date, the About page explains how to tell me.

The first guides are being checked on the ground and will appear here soon.

What is Knowledge Park?

Knowledge Park, Greater Noida (Uttar Pradesh), is where many of the city's colleges lie. Knowledge Park is not a single campus but consists of numbered sectors (Knowledge Park I, II, III and some more sectors) with universities, colleges and institutes. Sharda University and GL Bajaj Institute of Technology and Management are located in Knowledge Park III, while GCET (Galgotias College of Engineering and Technology) is located in Knowledge Park II.

This guide talks about Greater Noida and not Greater Noida West (Noida Extension). There is a lot of confusion about the two names.

The route to reach here is through the Aqua Line of Noida Metro, which begins at Noida Sector 51 (the change point from the Delhi Metro's Blue Line at Sector 52) and stops at Knowledge Park II and then Pari Chowk. Autos and e-rickshaws take care of the final distance to the colleges. Not sure which station to use? See the nearest metro station to Bennett University or the metro guide for Galgotias, Sharda and GL Bajaj.

Not just Knowledge Park

There are two major universities nearby that are outside Knowledge Park: Bennett University in Tech Zone II, and Galgotias University in Sector 17A on the Yamuna Expressway. Both are covered in this guide: Bennett has its own metro guide, and Galgotias University is in the Galgotias, Sharda and GL Bajaj metro guide. Food and PG pages will follow.

Additionally, GCET and Galgotias University are two different campuses at two different addresses. Please check which campus you are talking about before booking a cab.

Quick answers about Knowledge Park, Greater Noida

Is Knowledge Park in Noida or Greater Noida?

Greater Noida. Noida and Greater Noida are separate cities with separate development authorities, though both are in Gautam Buddh Nagar district, Uttar Pradesh. Sharda, GL Bajaj and GCET are in Knowledge Park II and III, which are not in Greater Noida West (Noida Extension). Knowledge Park V, though, is usually listed as part of Greater Noida West.

Which metro stations serve Knowledge Park?

Two neighbouring stations on the Noida Metro's Aqua Line: Knowledge Park II and Pari Chowk, the next stop towards Depot. Sharda University's own directions name both. Which one is closer depends on your college and gate, so check Getting here before you set off.

Which colleges are in Knowledge Park?

Among them are Sharda University and GL Bajaj (Knowledge Park III) and GCET (Knowledge Park II), plus many other institutes. Bennett University and Galgotias University are nearby but not inside Knowledge Park. The colleges in Knowledge Park guide lists 22 of them, with official contacts and the walk from the metro.

No. It's written by a Bennett student, but it isn't run, funded or endorsed by Bennett or any other college, PG or business.

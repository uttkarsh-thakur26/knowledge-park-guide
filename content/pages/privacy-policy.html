Who we are

Knowledge Park Guide (knowledgeparkguide.in) is an independent guide to student life in Knowledge Park and around, Greater Noida, written by Uttkarsh Thakur. There are no accounts to sign up for, comments are switched off, and we don't run ads.

Analytics (Google Analytics 4)

We use Google Analytics 4 to understand which guides are useful. It records things like the pages you view, how far you scroll, links you click to other sites, your approximate location (city level), your device and browser, and how you found the site. It sets cookies named _ga and _ga_* for this. We use this only as aggregate statistics; we don't try to identify individual visitors.

You can block these cookies in your browser settings, or install Google's Analytics opt-out add-on. Google's own use of this data is described in How Google uses information from sites that use its services.

Cloudflare

The site is delivered through Cloudflare, which processes technical data such as your IP address to serve pages quickly and block attacks.

Server logs

Like most websites, our server keeps standard access logs (IP address, page requested, browser, time) for security and troubleshooting. They are deleted automatically after 14 days.

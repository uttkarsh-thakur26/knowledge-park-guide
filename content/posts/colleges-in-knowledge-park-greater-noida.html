Quick answer: Knowledge Park II and Knowledge Park III in Greater Noida have more than 20 colleges, including GCET, GNIOT, NIET, IILM, BIMTECH, Lloyd, Sharda University, GL Bajaj, KCC, Amity, ITS, JIMS, Accurate, Dronacharya and Delhi Technical Campus. For all of them the nearest metro is Knowledge Park II on the Aqua Line. GCET is a 5-minute walk from the station and GNIOT about 10 minutes. The Knowledge Park III colleges are 1.7-3.9 km away, so you'll probably want an app bike or auto. Every address, phone number and email below is from the college's own website, checked on 26 October 2026.

All colleges at a glance

Getting from the metro to your college

Every college on this page is nearest to Knowledge Park II station on Noida Metro's Aqua Line. Pari Chowk, the next stop, is further from all of them. The cheapest ways to cover the last stretch:

  • Walk (free): GCET, GNIOT, BIMTECH and Mangalmay are 350 m to 1 km away, 5-15 minutes on foot. The Lloyd colleges, IILM and NIET are 1.3-1.6 km away, about 20 minutes.
  • App bike or auto: on Sunday evening, 25 October 2026, Uber quoted ₹21-32 for a bike ride from Knowledge Park II to GCET, Sharda or GL Bajaj, and ₹59 for an Uber Auto to GCET or Sharda. An Uber Go car to Sharda or GL Bajaj was about ₹120.
  • Shared e-rickshaw or auto: I haven't recorded these fares yet. Agree the price before you get in.
  • College buses: Sharda, GL Bajaj, KCC, JIMS and Delhi Technical Campus say they run buses for their own students. Routes and charges differ, so ask your college's transport office.

By NMRC's timetable (checked 29 September 2026), the last train towards Noida and Delhi leaves Knowledge Park II at 10:10 pm. Metro fares and first and last trains are in the Galgotias, Sharda and GL Bajaj metro guide, and the route from Delhi is in the Bennett metro guide.

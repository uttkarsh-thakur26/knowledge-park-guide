Quick answer: No metro station is within walking distance of Galgotias University. The nearest Aqua Line stations, Pari Chowk and Knowledge Park II, are both about 11 km away in a straight line; from Pari Chowk it's about 15 km by road, around 17 minutes by car, and an auto costs about ₹150-200. For Sharda University and GL Bajaj in Knowledge Park III, the nearest station is Knowledge Park II, 2.5-3 km by road. Galgotias College (GCET) is a different campus, about 550 m from Knowledge Park II.

All four campuses are in Greater Noida, not Greater Noida West. Sharda and GL Bajaj are in Knowledge Park III, GCET is in Knowledge Park II, and Galgotias University is outside Knowledge Park altogether, on the Yamuna Expressway.

Galgotias University or Galgotias College? Check which one first

Two campuses carry the Galgotias name, about 11 km apart, and they need different stations. Both belong to the Galgotias Educational Institutions group, but they are separate institutions:

  • Galgotias University (set up in 2011) is at Plot No. 2, Sector 17-A, Yamuna Expressway, opposite the Buddh International Circuit. This is the one far from the metro.
  • Galgotias College of Engineering and Technology (GCET) (set up in 2000, affiliated to AKTU) is at Plot No. 1, Knowledge Park II, a short walk from Knowledge Park II station.

If your admit letter or ID card says "University", read the next section. If it says GCET or Galgotias College, skip to GCET.

Galgotias University nearest metro station: Pari Chowk or Knowledge Park II

Knowledge Park II and Pari Chowk are neck and neck. Knowledge Park II is a few hundred metres closer in a straight line, but by road both routes join the Yamuna Expressway the same way, close to Pari Chowk, so the drive is almost the same length. Road distance also depends on direction: the expressway is divided, so the way back to the station is longer.

Google Maps puts Pari Chowk to Galgotias University at 15 km, about 17 minutes by car in usual traffic. That fastest route uses a toll road; the route without tolls takes about 21 minutes.

Last checked: 25 October 2026

Pari Chowk is the one to use. It's the stop Galgotias's own contact page sends visitors to, and when I checked on a Sunday evening, autos were waiting outside Gate 1 and Uber cars and bikes were 2-3 minutes away.

Pari Chowk to Galgotias University: fares

Last checked: 25 October 2026

Galgotias's contact page quotes an auto from Pari Chowk at around ₹50. The page isn't dated, and it routes you by DTC bus from ISBT Kashmiri Gate without mentioning the Aqua Line, which opened in January 2019, so treat that ₹50 as out of date. I haven't checked shared autos on this route or whether the university runs a bus from the metro; its call centre (0120-4370000, on the contact page) can tell you.

Sharda University nearest metro station

For Sharda University in Knowledge Park III, Knowledge Park II is the nearest station by every measure, a little under a kilometre closer by road than Pari Chowk. A 2025 Sharda conference page names both stations and says it's a 10-minute drive after that. Sharda is in Greater Noida, not Delhi, even though some searches ask for its "Delhi" metro station.

Google Maps' route from Knowledge Park II to Sharda is flagged as using a restricted or private road, so your driver may take a slightly different way.

GL Bajaj nearest metro station

GL Bajaj Institute of Technology and Management is at Plot No. 2, APJ Abdul Kalam Road, Knowledge Park III, under a kilometre from Sharda. Its nearest station is also Knowledge Park II. Its contact page doesn't give metro directions. Google Maps puts the entry gate 2.5 km by road from Knowledge Park II, a little more than the 2 km straight-line distance.

GCET (Galgotias College) nearest metro station

GCET is the closest of these campuses to the metro. Google Maps puts it 550 m by road from Knowledge Park II station: about a 5-minute walk or a 2-minute drive. Pari Chowk is about 2 km away by road. I haven't walked the route after dark myself, so if you arrive late, an app bike or auto for the short hop costs ₹21-59.

Knowledge Park II and Pari Chowk: fares, first and last trains

Both stations are on the Aqua Line, which runs from Noida Sector 51 to Depot. Knowledge Park II is the 15th stop after Sector 51 and Pari Chowk the 16th, so both fall in the same fare band.

Last checked: 29 September 2026

An NMRC smart card takes 10% off every journey. By NMRC's timetable, the ride from Sector 51 to Knowledge Park II takes about 35 minutes, with trains roughly every 7.5 minutes at peak times and every 10 minutes off-peak on weekdays. The two times to plan around are the late Sunday start and the last train towards Noida and Delhi, which leaves Knowledge Park II at 10:10 pm. Times can change, so check NMRC's train timings page on the day.

Getting to Knowledge Park II or Pari Chowk from Delhi

From Delhi, take the Blue Line towards Noida Electronic City to Noida Sector 52, cross the skywalk to Noida Sector 51 and ride the Aqua Line towards Depot. The route section of my Bennett metro guide covers the change at Sector 52 and Sector 51 and which tickets work on both networks; it's the same journey until you get off.

Why other pages give different answers

Search for the Galgotias University nearest metro station and you'll see answers from 1 km to 13 km. The 1 km figure comes from a page that has the university pinned about 10 km north of where it actually is, right next to Pari Chowk. The 13 km figure is in the right range for the road (Google Maps says 15 km from Pari Chowk) but usually comes without a fare, a time or a date. Some directions, including the university's own, still describe the pre-2019 bus route from Delhi, and at least one transit listing mixes up Galgotias University with GCET. The measurements on this page are dated so you can tell how fresh they are.

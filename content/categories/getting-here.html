Looking up the auto fare in Greater Noida? You probably need it for the last stretch, from a metro station to your college gate. The Aqua Line metro runs through Knowledge Park, with stations at Knowledge Park II and Pari Chowk, and getting from the station to campus is usually the trickier part. So far the guides below list auto fares checked in October 2026: about ₹150-200 from Pari Chowk to Bennett University, and about the same from Pari Chowk to Galgotias University. A table of shared e-rickshaw and auto fares to the other campus gates is being checked on the ground and will go up with the date each rate was checked. Until then, those fares aren't listed, because a guessed fare is worse than none.

Living in Greater Noida as a student gets easier once you know how the area fits together. Sharda University and GL Bajaj are in Knowledge Park III and GCET is in Knowledge Park II, while Bennett University (Tech Zone II) and Galgotias University (Sector 17A, Yamuna Expressway) are outside Knowledge Park itself. This is Greater Noida, not Greater Noida West, which people often mix up. For contacts, start with the colleges in Knowledge Park list: 22 colleges and institutes, each with its official admission and office contacts and how far it is from the metro.

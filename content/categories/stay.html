Looking for a PG in Knowledge Park, Greater Noida? This section is for students moving out of the hostel, freshers weighing a PG against a college hostel, and parents who want to check a place before paying a deposit. It covers Greater Noida, not Greater Noida West. The guides cover what actually matters: single or double sharing, whether food is included, the security deposit, in-time rules and how far you will be from your college gate. Every rent figure carries the date it was checked. An honest note: I live in the on-campus hostel at Bennett and have not rented a PG myself. PG details here come from visits and conversations with students who live outside campus. More on that on the about page. For how safe the area feels, see Living.
